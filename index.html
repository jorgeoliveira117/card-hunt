I-80 Card Hunt

Middletown, NY → Detroit, MI · Saturday, Oct 10

Google's fastest route: 604 miles, 8 h 54 min of driving, Ohio Turnpike tolls. Stops are in driving order with the freshest restock leads first.

Plan

Detroit, roughly–

ETAs add rough minutes per stop to Google's drive time, so use them for closing times, not to the minute. Nothing here guarantees stock: shelves change by the hour, and calling ahead is the only sure check.

Why these stores

Barnes & Noble

The strongest lead this week. 30th Celebration hit B&N shelves at launch on Sept 16, the second wave (booster bundles and mini tins) landed Oct 2, and collectors reported another B&N drop on Oct 5. Participating stores were also giving out a Mewtwo 30th Celebration promo with $15+ of Pokémon TCG, while supplies last.

Dollar General

A store arrival sheet reported by Restockd lists early-October shipments of 30th Celebration poster collections, Elite Trainer Boxes and ex boxes, and DG's own site lists the ETB and poster collection for in-store purchase. Each store gets one truck a week on its own day, and rural stores off I-80 see far less competition than suburban Targets.

Meijer

Named alongside Target, Walmart and B&N in collector posts about 30th Celebration restocks. Open until midnight, which makes it the safest bet for the late Toledo–Detroit stretch.

Target & Walmart

Both carry all three sets and supply is moving: Target restocked the 30th Celebration ETB and Ascended Heroes tech sticker collections online on Oct 9, and Walmart ran online drawings Oct 7–8. In stores, a third-party vendor fills the card aisle on each store's own schedule, and Friday is the most-reported Target vendor day, so a Saturday visit can turn up leftovers or nothing.

Best Buy

Carries the line but sells much of it online. Look up the exact product in the Best Buy app with that store selected before walking in.

Latest signal

  • The long shot. Recent activity has been online (Walmart's Oct 8 drawing added the Premium Figure Collection). Dollar General has turned up Prismatic ETBs before, so glance at every DG rack.

At each stop

  1. Call the Barnes & Nobles before he reaches them and ask whether they have 30th Celebration out. Their numbers are on each stop.
  2. At Dollar General, check the card rack, endcaps, the side panels of aisles and the registers, then ask whether this week's truck has been unloaded yet. Restockd's app has an In Store tab with store-reported counts for DG's 30th Celebration items by ZIP code.
  3. Every Ohio Turnpike exit is a toll plaza each way (E-ZPass works), so each Ohio stop costs about five extra minutes.
  4. Expect purchase limits on new Pokémon product, and skip anything from the "not out yet" list sitting on a shelf at a markup.
  5. After leaving a stop, tap "Leaving here now" so the ETAs for the rest of the drive update.
